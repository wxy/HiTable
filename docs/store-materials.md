# HiTable 0.2.2 — Store materials

For the maintainer preparing the Chrome Web Store listing. This bundle does not submit or publish anything.

- `screenshots/<locale>/`: upload these three PNGs, in order, for that language: green selection, blue selection/menu, settings. All are 1280×800.
- `screenshots/global-en/`: separate English fallback group for the global listing.
- `promos/small-440x280.png`: global small promotional tile.
- `promos/marquee-1400x560.png`: global top/marquee promotional tile.
- `descriptions/<locale>.txt`: complete localized store description, each under 4,000 characters.
- `source/promo-design.md`: design notes. The two promotional images are original brand illustrations; the screenshots are genuine extension captures.

Use the separate `hitable-0.2.2-store.zip` as the extension upload. Do not upload this materials ZIP as the extension package. Final review submission is performed by the maintainer.
